# Destination Guide: New York, United States

## Visa and entry requirements

Travelers from Visa Waiver Program countries (including the UK, most of the
EU, Japan, and Australia) can enter the US visa-free for stays of up to 90
days for tourism or business, provided they have an approved ESTA
(Electronic System for Travel Authorization) obtained before departure.
Travelers from other countries generally need a B-1/B-2 visa. A passport
valid for the duration of the stay is required.

## Climate

New York has four distinct seasons. Spring (March-May) is mild and
variable. Summer (June-August) is hot and humid, with occasional
thunderstorms. Autumn (September-November) is mild and considered one of
the best times to visit. Winter (December-February) is cold, with
occasional snowstorms and temperatures often below freezing.

## Customs

Tipping is expected and customary: 18-20% at sit-down restaurants, and a
dollar or two per drink at bars. Walking pace on sidewalks tends to be
brisk, especially in Manhattan; standing still in the middle of a busy
sidewalk is considered inconsiderate. Jaywalking is common but always
carries some risk from traffic.

## Packing advice

Pack layers, since indoor heating and air conditioning can be intense
regardless of season. A warm, windproof coat is essential in winter given
wind chill between buildings. Comfortable walking shoes are a must given
how much of the city is explored on foot or via subway stairs.
