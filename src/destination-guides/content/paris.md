# Destination Guide: Paris, France

## Visa and entry requirements

Travelers from the US, UK, Canada, and Australia can enter France (and the
wider Schengen Area) visa-free for stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day
period, for tourism or business. A passport valid for at least 3 months
beyond the departure date from the Schengen Area is required. Starting in
2026, ETIAS pre-travel authorization is required for visa-exempt
nationalities.

## Climate

Paris has a temperate climate. Spring (March-May) is mild with occasional
rain. Summer (June-August) is warm, with average highs in the low-to-mid
20s Celsius; heatwaves can occur in July and August. Autumn (September-
November) is cool and often rainy. Winter (December-February) is cold and
damp, occasionally with light snow.

## Customs

Greet shopkeepers and staff with "Bonjour" before starting a conversation;
skipping this is considered impolite. Tipping is not mandatory since
service is included by law, but rounding up or leaving small change for
good service is appreciated. Meals, especially dinner, are treated as
leisurely social occasions rather than something to rush.

## Packing advice

Pack a versatile layering system: a light jacket or trench coat works for
most of the year, with a warmer coat needed in winter. Comfortable walking
shoes are essential given how walkable the city is and its cobblestone
streets. A compact umbrella is useful year-round given the frequent light
rain.
