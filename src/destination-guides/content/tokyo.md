# Destination Guide: Tokyo, Japan

## Visa and entry requirements

Travelers from the US, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia can enter Japan visa-free
for stays of up to 90 days for tourism or business. A passport valid for the
duration of the stay is required. Visitors must complete Japan's electronic
disembarkation/customs form (Visit Japan Web) before arrival to speed up
immigration and customs processing.

## Climate

Tokyo has four distinct seasons. Spring (March-May) is mild with cherry
blossoms in late March/early April. Summer (June-August) is hot and humid,
with a rainy season in June. Autumn (September-November) is mild and dry.
Winter (December-February) is cool and dry, rarely dropping below freezing.

## Customs

Tipping is not customary and can be considered rude in restaurants, taxis,
and hotels. Remove shoes before entering homes, traditional inns (ryokan),
and some restaurants. It is considered impolite to eat or drink while
walking. Bowing is a common greeting; a slight nod is sufficient for
visitors.

## Packing advice

Pack layers for temperature swings between indoor heating/air conditioning
and outdoor weather. Comfortable slip-on shoes are useful given how often
shoes are removed indoors. During the summer rainy season, pack a compact
umbrella. Cash is still widely used, so carry yen alongside cards.
